Your back button deletes the pages you leave behind. Trailhead keeps them.

Go back, click something new, and normal history throws away the path you were on. Trailhead keeps a tree instead, so the page you left stays one click away. It records the pages you visit, locally, on your device.

How it works

Trailhead runs quietly in the background and shows its tree whenever you open it.

  1. Browse as usualTrailhead notes each page and which page led to it. Pages open in new tabs hang off the page you clicked.
  2. Go back, then click something newInstead of vanishing, the page you left stays on your trail as a branch.
  3. Open the side panelRestore a page you left behind, see the whole trail as a list or graph, or open the full-page map.

What you get

A small set of tools built around one idea: the pages you leave are still yours.

Left behind, one click from Restore

Pages you backed out of are shelved at the top of the side panel. Restore one in a new tab, or press Alt+Shift+B to bring back the most recent one.

Your trail as a graph

See every branch of what you browsed. The page you are on is highlighted, and links opened in a new tab appear as dashed branches.

Every tab on one map

The full-page map draws all your open tabs and their trails together, one colour per tab, so you can see where each tab came from.

Search across all your trails

Find any page you visited by its title or address, including pages on trails whose tabs you have closed.

Pin it, note it, park it

Star pages or add short notes so they are kept. Park a trail to close its tabs and reopen them later from the Saved view.

Everything stays on your device

Trailhead has no account, no server and no analytics. It makes no network requests of its own.

What it keeps

  • Page addresses (including any search terms in them), titles and visit times
  • How pages connect, and which tab is on which page
  • Pins, notes and settings you create

What it never records

  • Incognito windows
  • Page content, form fields, passwords or keystrokes
  • Sites you exclude, or anything while recording is paused

You are in control

Pause recording, exclude sites, choose how long trails are kept (30 days by default), or erase it all: open the side panel, choose the gear icon, then Forget everything.
