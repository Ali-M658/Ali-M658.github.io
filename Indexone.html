Trailhead Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

In short: Trailhead works entirely on your device. It has no server and no account, and it never sends your data anywhere.

Trailhead is a browser extension that keeps a tree of the pages you visit so you can get back to pages you left behind.

What Trailhead keeps on your device

  • The addresses (URLs), titles and visit times of pages you visit in normal (non-incognito) windows, and how they connect.
  • Which tabs are open and which page each one is on.
  • Optional: your scroll position on a page, only if you turn on "Remember scroll position".
  • Optional: pins, notes and parked trails that you create yourself.
  • Your settings, including sites you choose never to record.

This data is kept only in your browser's local extension storage. It is not uploaded, synced or shared.

What Trailhead does not do

  • It does not send data to the developer or any third party, and it makes no network requests of its own.
  • It does not record incognito windows.
  • It does not read or store page content, form fields, passwords or keystrokes.
  • It does not sell data, use it for advertising, or use it to decide creditworthiness.
  • It does not load or run remote code.

Your control

  • Pause recording, exclude sites, or choose how long trails are kept, in the settings.
  • Delete a single branch, or choose "Forget everything" to erase all stored data.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes its stored data.

Permissions

Trailhead requests browser permissions only to build and show your trail locally. The optional all-sites permission is requested only if you turn on scroll memory, and it is removed when you turn that setting off.
